AAAI 2026 Fall Symposium Series

Aligning with Whom?

Pluriversal and Paraconsistent Frameworks for Value Alignment in AI Systems

November 5–7, 2026  ·  Westin Arlington Gateway, Arlington, Virginia, USA

01About the Symposium

Current alignment methods (RLHF, Constitutional AI, DPO) treat alignment as aggregating diverse human values into a single utility function fixed at training time. When principles are elicited from pluralistic populations, structurally irreducible contradictions emerge, and they are resolved, in deployment, by majority vote, which silences minorities, indigenous epistemologies, and Global South value frameworks.

The question is political and epistemic before it is technical: aligning with whom?

This symposium convenes researchers from formal logic, AI safety, cross-cultural value elicitation, and Global South AI governance to ask whether current architectures can handle genuine value pluralism, and what alternatives exist. The working thesis is that paraconsistent, three-component belief frameworks (Belnap–Dunn logic, Dempster–Shafer belief functions, imprecise probability) can represent irreducible value contradictions without collapsing them into a single ordering.

02Topics

  • Formal frameworks for value indeterminacy (paraconsistent, Belnap–Dunn, Dempster–Shafer, imprecise probability)
  • The majority-vote problem in Constitutional AI
  • Cross-cultural value elicitation (Pol.is, deliberative mini-publics)
  • Global South and decolonial perspectives on alignment
  • Culturally sensitive evaluation benchmarks
  • Uncertainty quantification in LLMs (epistemic vs. aleatory)
  • Pedagogy of indeterminacy and AI literacy

03Format

A 2.5-day program designed for cross-disciplinary synthesis:

  • Short-paper presentations (25 min + 10 min Q&A)
  • Two keynote-anchored panels: formal frameworks; cross-cultural empirics
  • Three parallel working groups producing one-page position statements
  • A closing synthesis plenary toward a shared research agenda

Target size: 45–60 participants. Approximately 20% of slots are reserved for non-presenting attendees from outside the organizing communities.

04Submissions

We invite full papers (6–8 pages) and short / position papers and extended abstracts (2–4 pages) in AAAI format. Both mature results and work in progress are welcome. At least one author per accepted paper must attend and present in person.
